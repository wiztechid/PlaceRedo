HOW PLACEREDO THINKS

Useful assumptions, visible by design.

Coverage. V1 uses roughly 350 sq ft per gallon per coat and deducts standard door/window openings. Ceiling and trim are added only when selected.

Prices. Paint tiers, prep supplies and labor use broad US planning assumptions—not live retailer prices or a local contractor quote.

Next. Local cost adjustment, detailed trim/door scope, tax, primer logic and actual-cost feedback will be added as the model matures.
